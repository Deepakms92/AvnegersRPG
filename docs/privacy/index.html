WellPing Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

In short: WellPing doesn't collect, send or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. Everything the app saves stays on your phone.

What WellPing stores on your device

  • Your settings: reminder interval, whether reminders and walking detection are on.
  • Your timer: when you last moved, and any active snooze.
  • Game progress: XP, level, moves, daily quest progress, streak and badges.

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We (the developer) have no access to it, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Step count and motion data

If you allow it, WellPing reads your phone's step counter (Physical Activity on Android, Motion & Fitness on iOS) to notice when you walk, so your sitting timer resets automatically. Step counts are used on the device only, at the moment they are read. They are not stored as history and never leave your phone. You can turn this off in the app (Game settings → Walking restores energy) or in your phone's settings at any time.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No server is involved and no push-notification token is created or sent anywhere.

Device backups

Your phone's own backup features may include the app's saved data in your personal backup: Android's automatic backup to your Google account, or an iCloud/computer backup on iPhone. These backups are controlled by you and by Google or Apple under their privacy policies; WellPing's developer cannot access them.

Data shared with third parties

None. WellPing contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling WellPing deletes all of its data from your device. On Android you can also use Settings → Apps → WellPing → Storage → Clear data. (Device backups you've made are managed in your Google or Apple account settings.)

Children

WellPing is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of WellPing changes how data is handled (for example, by adding optional sign-in and cloud backup), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
